# Plan: provisional-boundary trading for Selene, Helios and Typhon

Created 2026-10-08 at the user's instruction ("it's a critical fix for an existing infrastructure issue at the broker, and has proved itself in the past with Prometheus; it should be part of the other 3 engines as well. Let the margins come from the history measurement. The rigor is non negotiable."). Prometheus's engine has it; Selene, Helios and Typhon were built with `ProvisionalSpec(False)` ("not modelled by the parity backtest; nothing calibrated it", `plans/hestia-p7-selene-engine.md`). Prometheus's own value, the 0.15% `provisional_margin_pct`, is a placeholder that was never calibrated (`plans/selene-production.md` §1.9). Nothing here changes Prometheus.

**Status (2026-10-08): written BEFORE any measurement; sections 1 and 2 are the pre-registration. Results are appended below it (section 5) and never edit sections 1 to 4. Section 7 records the user's decision the same day to set the margins at the typical size instead and to switch the feature on for all four engines; it supersedes sections 2 point 3 and 6.**

## 0. What it is, verified in the code

At a 15-minute boundary, Hestia (`hestia_core/live_data.py`, `_after_merge`) checks the window of one-minute candles it holds; if there are fewer than 15 it marks the bar deferred and sends the engine a `ProvisionalBar` built from the websocket tick accumulator (`tick_acc`, harvested at the minute tick; ticks are `get_ohlc` chunks of last-traded-price updates). The real bar follows when the candles arrive, or at the cutoff (`deferred_bar_cutoff_min`, 1 minute) built from what is on hand. The engine (`prometheus_engine/engine.py`, `_on_provisional`) acts only if (a) the provisional bar's supertrend flips and (b) its close clears the PREVIOUS bar's supertrend by more than `provisional_margin_pct` (percent of close); it then sends an ordinary flip or entry request, remembers the boundary, and when the real bar arrives `_reconcile_provisional` logs CONFIRMED, or on a disagreement raises a critical alert, latches provisional action off for the session, and does not reverse the position. It is not an early entry: it fires after the bar has ended, only when the candle window is incomplete.

Two facts from the code that shape the measurement:
- **A flip depends only on the close crossing the previous bar's supertrend** (`hestia_core/indicators.py`, `compute_st`: trend turns bullish if `close > prev upper band`, bearish if `close < prev lower band`, and the previous bar's supertrend value IS that band when the trend is the one being left). The provisional bar's high and low only change the NEW supertrend value. So a provisional action and the real bar can disagree only if their CLOSES fall on opposite sides of the previous supertrend.
- **"Incomplete" is not only an AB1021 condition.** Angel One omits one-minute candles in which nothing traded, so on a thin contract any 15-minute window containing an untraded minute holds fewer than 15 candles at the boundary and triggers the provisional path (the GOLDPETAL 14:15-14:30 bar on 2026-10-07 was exactly that: minute 14:18 had no trade). The trigger frequency per instrument is measured in section 5.

## 1. Pre-registered measurement (fixed before running)

**Data.** The four instruments' one-minute Fyers history, real un-adjusted prices, as the Janus and engine backtests load it (`prometheus_backtest/data_loader_p3` helpers: contract calendar, effective contract per date, per-contract files), through the latest data, per instrument from its own start date. Fifteen-minute bars are built per contract on the clock (windows at :00, :15, :30, :45), and the supertrend is computed per contract with `hestia_core.indicators.compute_st` and the engine's own parameters: SILVERMIC (10, 2.5), GOLDPETAL (10, 3.5), NATGASMINI (10, 3.0), and CRUDEOILM (10, 2.5) as a reference only. Only bars on dates when that contract is the effective trading contract are used.

**The close error and its bound U.** The provisional close is the last websocket price at the boundary; the real close is the last traded price of the window's final candle. For a bar, let `U` be, as a percent of the bar's real close, the larger of (i) the high-low range of the window's final traded minute and (ii) the absolute move from that minute's close to the next minute's open, the latter counted only when the next minute opens within 3 minutes of the boundary (a tick that arrives just after the boundary). Assumption A (below) makes `|tick close - real close| <= U`.

**Assumption A, stated because it cannot be measured here.** The engine skips provisional action while the feed is stale (new gate, section 3), where stale means no tick received for more than `feed_stale_after_s` = 30 s. With a fresh feed the last price was received within 30 s of the boundary, hence is a price traded in the final minute (or just after the boundary). The feed stores only the time a tick was RECEIVED (`websocket_feed._last_tick_ts`), not an exchange timestamp, so a lagging feed that keeps delivering old prices would look fresh; that case is not detectable with today's instrumentation and is the residual risk. The 26 observations from the Prometheus logs (section 4 of the results) are used as a consistency check of the bound, never to calibrate.

**Populations.**
- **Risk population.** Every bar (valid supertrend on the previous bar, window end inside the session, final minute traded: volume > 0) on which the REAL close did NOT flip the trend. For such a bar let `d` be the distance, as a percent of close, from the real close to the previous supertrend on the side it stayed. A harmful provisional action needs the tick close to cross the line by more than the margin `m` while the real close stayed `d` short of it, i.e. an error larger than `d + m`; with `|error| <= U` that is possible only if `U - d > m`. Define `r = max(0, U - d)` per bar. Real flips are NOT in this population: if the real bar flips, a provisional flip agrees with it and a provisional non-flip only waits.
- **Coverage population.** Every REAL flip bar on a traded final minute. `coverage(m)` is the share with clearance `|real close - previous supertrend| / close * 100 > m`. This is what the feature buys: the share of real flips it could act on early.

**The rule.** `m*` is the smallest value on a 0.01% grid that is at least the maximum of `r` over the risk population of the FIT period (bars before 2026-01-01), i.e. zero worst-case harmful actions in the fit period. It is verified out of sample on the 2026 bars: the count of bars with `r > m*` must be 0. If it is not 0, `m*` is recomputed as the maximum over all history and the failure is reported, never hidden. Reported alongside, not used to choose: the 99th, 99.9th and 99.99th percentiles of `r`, the top 10 bars by `r` with timestamps (each inspected to say whether it is a genuine market event or a data error; a bar is excluded only with a demonstrated data error and the exclusion is listed), `coverage(m)` at `m*` and at the percentiles, and the same numbers for CRUDEOILM against its current 0.15%.

**What is not done with the result.** If `coverage(m*)` is low, that is reported with the trade-off. The rule is not loosened afterwards, and Prometheus's value is not changed.

## 2. Pre-registered acceptance criteria for the code

1. Provisional handling ported into `selene_engine`, `helios_engine` and `typhon_engine` from `prometheus_engine`, keeping every Prometheus guard: supertrend warm-up, roll armed, a flip or request pending, the bar at or after the close, the margin check against the previous supertrend, the session latch, and the reconcile branch placed ahead of normal flip handling in `_on_bar`.

Config fields `provisional_enabled` and `provisional_margin_pct` per engine, the margin set from section 5, and **`provisional_enabled` False by default in every engine config**, with a test pinning that default. 4. Tests: Prometheus's provisional cases ported through each engine's fake-Hestia helper, plus the staleness gate, the bar-at-close case, the roll and pending-flip skips, the latch, the default-off pin, and the edge case of a real bar finalised at the cutoff with its final minute still missing (what the reconcile then compares). Every behaviour broken on purpose to confirm a test fails.
5. The engines' replay tests rerun with provisional switched on: unchanged on complete data. Full suite run (the Helios replay test is the known parked failure).
6. Documentation in the same change: this plan, `plans/selene-production.md` §1.9, `plans/hestia-p7-selene-engine.md`, the Helios and Typhon plans, `hestia_core/README.md`, the engine docstrings, and the hestia-qc skill (provisional actions and reconciles added to its engine checks).
7. Nothing committed or pushed without the user's explicit say; every Delos read stated as read-only first.

## 3. Staleness gate: design

Each engine tracks `self.feed_stale` (a set of contract tokens): added on `FeedStale`, removed on `FeedRecovered`. `_on_provisional` returns without acting when the engine's trading contract is in the set, logging why. Skipping falls back to the existing behaviour (wait for the real bar).

## 5. Results of the pre-registered measurement (2026-10-08)

Code: `research/provisional_margin/` (`margin_configs.py`, `measure_margins.py`, `trigger_frequency.py`; tests `tests/test_provisional_margin_measure.py`, 15, eight behaviours broken on purpose and caught after a downtrend case was added for the one the first set missed). Outputs in `research/provisional_margin/outputs/`: `margin_results.csv`, `grid_<SYMBOL>.csv`, `top_r_<SYMBOL>.csv` and `trigger_frequency.csv` are committed as the record of the result; `bars_<SYMBOL>.csv` (every 15-minute bar with its signal columns, about 255,000 rows in all) is gitignored and regenerated in about 2.5 minutes by `measure_margins.py`. Data: Fyers one-minute history to the end of each instrument's latest contract (2026-08-31 SILVERMIC, 2026-09-30 GOLDPETAL, 2026-09-25 NATGASMINI, 2026-09-21 CRUDEOILM), per contract on the dates it was effective. Section 1 was fixed before the run and was not edited after it.

**The pre-registered rule's output** (m* = smallest 0.01% grid value at or above the fit-period maximum of r, bars before 2026-01-01; checked on 2026):

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Fit-period maximum r | 0.454% | 0.709% | 1.058% | 0.940% |
| Out-of-sample violations at that m* | 0 | 0 | 0 | **1** (the 2026-08-17 15:45 bar, r 1.058%) |
| **m\*** | **0.46%** | **0.71%** | **1.06%** | 1.06% (all history, the check failed) |
| r at 99% / 99.9% / 99.99% | 0.019 / 0.094 / 0.263 | 0.009 / 0.071 / 0.267 | 0.043 / 0.235 / 0.447 | 0.033 / 0.163 / 0.454 |

The top bars that set each maximum were inspected minute by minute (`top_r_<SYMBOL>.csv`): all are genuine market events, none a data error, so nothing was excluded. Examples: 2023-12-01 16:14, crude (6393 to 6312, 9,173 lots) and silver (77,700 to 77,310, 43,371 lots) in the same minute; crude 2026-08-17 15:59 (7894 to 7807, 1,958 lots, then trading on at the lower level); natural gas 2023-11-22 22:29 to 22:30 (251.0 to 254.2, 634 lots, then trading on at the higher level); gold 2022-01-20 22:43 (4819 to 4766 and back to 4814 on 1,309 and 1,761 lots).

**Sensitivity, reported and not used to choose.** Dropping the post-boundary gap term (range of the final minute only): SILVERMIC 0.46%, GOLDPETAL 0.71%, CRUDEOILM 1.06% unchanged, NATGASMINI falls from 1.06% to 0.65% (coverage 15.1% all years). Worst-case harmful bars (r above the margin), per year, over the history: at 0.15% (Prometheus's current value) SILVERMIC 4.8, GOLDPETAL 2.6, NATGASMINI 34.8, CRUDEOILM 15.0; at the 99.9th percentile 14.2 / 11.6 / 14.1 / 13.6; at the 99.99th percentile 1.5 / 1.2 / 1.4 / 1.4; at m* zero by construction. These are counts of bars where the bound ALLOWS a harmful action, i.e. where a tick-close error as large as the whole final minute's range would be enough; they are not expected harmful actions.

**What the rule costs.** m* is far above the median clearance of a real flip (0.05% to 0.2%), so it lets a provisional action fire on only about 2% to 8% of real flips (22% of silver's in the volatile 2026). Prometheus's own 0.15% would cover 17% to 60% of flips on these instruments but is not safe against the worst-case bound: crude has 15 bars a year where the bound allows harm at that margin. The bound is deliberately worst-case; actual tick closes track the candle closely: of 26 Prometheus provisional evaluations in the logs that could be matched to their real bar (3 weeks of Hestia and 5 weeks of the standalone process), 25 had provisional close equal to real close and one differed by 3 points (0.03%); the only flip among them (2026-09-08 15:00, bearish) was confirmed by the real bar 58 seconds later. That is 26 evaluations on one instrument and says nothing about the other three, which is why section 6 puts a shadow stage before enabling.

**How often the path runs** (`trigger_frequency.csv`, Angel One nightly files since 2026-02-01 or the contract's start; the live cache also holds REST failures, so these are lower bounds): windows with fewer than 15 candles at the boundary per session: SILVERMIC 1.29 (2.3% of windows), GOLDPETAL 3.98 (7.0%), NATGASMINI 2.48 (4.4%), CRUDEOILM 0.39 (0.7%). Real flips falling in such windows: 6 of 116 (SILVERMIC), 4 of 44 (GOLDPETAL), 0 of 87 (NATGASMINI), 0 of 258 (CRUDEOILM). So on the thin contracts the provisional path is evaluated several times a session, mostly on quiet minutes, but a flip coinciding with an incomplete window is rare (about 0.09 per session for silver and gold before the margin, none seen for natural gas); at m* the expected acted events are around 1 to 2 a year for silver and under 1 a year for gold. For the quiet-minute case the real bar is finalised at the one-minute cutoff, so the saving is at most about a minute plus any REST retry time.

## 6. Decision taken on these numbers

The three engines get the feature with **the pre-registered m\* as their `provisional_margin_pct`** (SILVERMIC 0.46, GOLDPETAL 0.71, NATGASMINI 1.06), `provisional_enabled` **False**, and a new `provisional_shadow` mode (default False) that turns Hestia's provisional bars on for the engine WITHOUT acting on them, logging every evaluation and, when the real bar arrives, the provisional close against the real close and what the action would have been. Shadow is the instrument the plan's own section 1.9 asked for: it measures the tick-versus-candle close error on all four instruments in live conditions, with no order ever sent. Enabling real action, and whether m* can be relaxed from measured shadow errors (for example the observed maximum error with a stated safety factor), are the user's calls after a stretch of shadow data; nothing here changes Prometheus's 0.15%, which on this evidence sits below the worst-case bound (flagged for the user, not changed).

## 7. Decision (user, 2026-10-08): typical-size margins, on for all four engines; supersedes sections 2.3 and 6

The pre-registered worst-case margins (section 5) kept the feature almost inert: they let it act on only 2% to 8% of real flips, so a flip that cleared the previous supertrend comfortably would still wait for the real bar, which is the case the feature exists for ("catch flips and prevent big losses on exits or big slips on entries"). The user's direction: the margin is the mean or median size of the noise, not the worst case; it is a practical setting, not a mathematical-accuracy exercise; the feature goes live; and Prometheus follows the same logic as the others. The section 5 rule and its numbers remain as the record and are no longer the setting.

**The setting.** `provisional_margin_pct` = the mean high-low range of the final traded minute of a 15-minute bar over each instrument's history (the bound U of section 1, the typical wobble in the last minute before the boundary), as a percent of price, rounded up to 0.01%:

| | mean U | **margin** | median U | real flips covered, all years / 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SILVERMIC (Selene) | 0.049% | **0.05%** | 0.038% | 63% / 81% |
| GOLDPETAL (Helios) | 0.031% | **0.04%** | 0.023% | 54% / 64% |
| NATGASMINI (Typhon) | 0.109% | **0.11%** | 0.088% | 68% / 63% |
| CRUDEOILM (Prometheus) | 0.068% | **0.07%** | 0.053% | 64% / 74% |

`provisional_enabled` is True in all four engine configs; `provisional_shadow` stays False (it only has effect when enabled is False). Prometheus's own value moves from 0.15% to 0.07% in `prometheus_engine/engine_configs.py` and in `prometheus_production/prometheus_configs.py` (kept equal by the pin test; the standalone process is rollback only).

**Where Prometheus's 0.15% came from (checked in the history, 2026-10-08).** It was a placeholder from the day the feature was built (commit `d9a98f6`, 2026-09-04: "uncalibrated margin placeholder", the verdict shadow-logged "so the margin can eventually be calibrated on real agreement data rather than a guess"; the config comment and `plans/prometheus-phase3-production.md` section 12a both say "PLACEHOLDER, not calibrated"). It was switched on the same day for a dry-run stress test and was never calibrated afterwards. The only real exercise, 2026-09-08 15:00, cleared the line by 0.91%. The 2026-09-28 change fixed what the margin is measured against (the previous supertrend) and noted that 0.15% held back about 44% of crude flips (a description of its effect). On the data: 0.15% sits at the 92.6th percentile of crude's final-minute range (median 0.053%, mean 0.068%) and covers 40% of real flips; the mean-range value 0.07% covers 64%.

**The nets, in order.** A provisional bar is built in `live_data._after_merge`, which runs only after the whole fetch job for the poll, and that job includes Angel One's retries and then the Fyers rescue (`fetch_one_minute_window` with its fallback). So a provisional bar exists only when the 15-minute window is still incomplete after the rescue has had its turn (or when a quiet minute leaves it legitimately short, where the tick close is simply the last trade). Provisional trading is the net below the Fyers rescue, not a path beside it.

**Safety kept.** The staleness gate (the three newer engines), reconciliation against the real bar with a critical alert and a session latch on disagreement, the critical alert when the real bar never arrives, and the always-on close check logging (provisional close against real close, per evaluation), which is the data for adjusting these numbers later. A disagreement was never meant to happen often: it alerts, latches provisional action off for the session and does not reverse the position.

**Going live is a pull and a Hestia restart** (config is read at start). The measurement and the section 5 results are unchanged.
