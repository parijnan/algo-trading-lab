# Janus: Camarilla pivot intraday research (MCX)

Research only: nothing here touches live code, Hestia, Delos or the engines' configs. Plan and phases: `plans/janus-camarilla-research.md`. Instruments: CRUDEOILM, SILVERMIC, GOLDPETAL, NATGASMINI (the four the engines trade, liquidity already confirmed; any of them could later be hosted by Hestia).

## Files

- `janus_configs.py`: every parameter (symbols, data start per instrument, the shared Fyers data void (filled 2026-10-05, now `None`: nothing is skipped), level factors, first-passage pairs). No magic numbers elsewhere.
- `janus_levels.py`: pure function, previous session high/low/close to R1..R7 and S1..S7 (R1-R4 = C + R x 1.1/{12,6,4,2}, R5 = (H/L) x C, R6 = R5 + 1.168 x (R5 - R4), R7 = R6 + 1.168 x (R6 - R5), S mirrored). R7 has no standard definition: it continues the same geometric ladder and is a convention, not a published formula.
- `janus_data.py`: one record per session with that session's 1-minute bars of the effective (early-roll) contract and the levels from the SAME contract's own previous session, so a roll day never mixes two contracts. Fyers history only, real un-adjusted prices; sessions in the shared Fyers void (2026-04-01 to 2026-06-29) are skipped, never filled. The Angel One tail (2026-09-02 onward) is not included yet.
- `janus_events.py`: per session and level, the first touch and what follows (first passage between an outward and an inward level, excursions, session close versus the level), in an upper frame so one piece of logic serves both sides. A bar that reaches both levels is reported as ambiguous, never guessed; a gap beyond a level at the open is flagged `at_open`, not counted as a touch.
- `levels_export.py`: prints the actual levels. `outputs/levels_<SYMBOL>.csv` (gitignored) has one row per session: the previous H/L/C, S7..R7 as numbers, the session's own open/high/low/close, and the outermost R and S level it reached. `outputs/levels_latest.txt` (tracked) has the levels for the session after the last one in each instrument's Fyers data, which ends at that contract's last session, not at today.
- `phase0_descriptive.py`: runs all four instruments, writes `outputs/phase0_events_<SYMBOL>.csv` (gitignored) and `outputs/phase0_summary.txt`.
- `janus_setups.py` / `thirds_feasibility.py` (2026-10-11): touch-entry detection (armed by an earlier bar, no entry in the first minute) and the first-passage path of one trade (T1, T2, T3, retrace to the entry price, re-bought lot, stop trailed to cost or to T1), and the descriptive feasibility study of equal thirds with a trail and re-entry; summary in `outputs/thirds_feasibility_summary.txt`, findings in `plans/janus-camarilla-research.md` section 9a. Parameters `SETUPS` and `TRAIL_SAME_BAR` in `janus_configs.py`.
- `janus_engine.py` / `thirds_pnl.py` (2026-10-11): day simulation of the decided rules (four setups, thirds, original stop, optional re-buy and counter trade, no cap) walking each bar's assumed price path, and the arm comparison A0..A3 under two bar orders with costs; parity check `thirds_pnl.py --parity`. Summary `outputs/thirds_pnl_summary.txt`, findings in `plans/janus-camarilla-research.md` section 9b. Parameters `ABS_SETUPS`, `ARMS`, `BAR_ORDERS`, cut-offs, `COSTS_PCT`, `TICK_THROUGH` in `janus_configs.py`.
- Entry modes (2026-10-11): `touch` and `close_limit` (close-armed watcher with a limit order at the level; 1, 5 and 15 minute closes; realistic retest fill versus the unachievable literal fill), see `ENTRY_SCENARIOS`/`RUN_PLAN` and plan section 9c. Run a symbol with `python janus_backtest/thirds_pnl.py SYMBOL` (the four in parallel), then `--report`.
- Range-based levels (2026-10-12, plan `plans/janus-range-levels.md`): `janus_levels.range_states` (setter-candle range over one contract's daily bars) and `janus_data.load_sessions(symbol, level_source=...)` with `LEVEL_SOURCE`/`LEVEL_SOURCES`/`RANGE_MIN_BARS` in `janus_configs.py`; the session records keep the control levels in `levels_prev` and the range state in `range`. `range_levels_descriptive.py` writes `outputs/range_levels_descriptive.txt` (no P&L). Default stays `previous_session`, so every earlier result is unchanged.
- `range_levels_export.py` (2026-10-15): the range-basis levels session by session, `outputs/range_levels_<SYMBOL>.csv` (gitignored; run with a symbol, e.g. `CRUDEOILM`). Columns: `range_id` (one number per range, in order of first use; a range is one contract's setter candle plus its wick extensions, so the id stays while the bounds widen), the range (high, low, setter date, age, extensions, width), S7..R7, the previous-session control R3/S3/R4/S4, the session's open/high/low/close, and `n_touched`, `max_R_touched`, `max_S_touched`, `levels_touched` (a bar's high at or above an R level, or low at or below an S level; C excluded). `outputs/range_level_touches_<SYMBOL>.csv` has the first-touch minute of every touched level (one row per session and level, in time order, with minutes from the open); a session that opens beyond a level touches it on its first bar. `load_sessions(..., live_tail=True)` (used by this export only; every other Janus script keeps Fyers only) reads a contract with no Fyers file yet from the live Angel One tree, keeping rows from `LIVE_OWN_CONTRACT_FROM` (2026-09-02, when the live files hold that contract's own prices); those sessions have `data_source` = `angelone_tail`. Once a contract expires and its Fyers file is downloaded, re-running the export replaces its tail sessions with Fyers ones. Because the tail supplies the October contract's data, the early roll to it now lands on 2026-09-14 instead of falling back to the September contract.
- State machine (2026-10-20, plan `plans/janus-state-machine.md`): `janus_machine.py` is the engine's event walk rebuilt around the trade (the engine stays untouched as the control): the stop belongs to the trade so it can be trailed, every lot and trade keeps its MFE and MAE with the minute each was reached (measured from the fill to the exit), every trade gets a per-minute log, and every session an ordered event log (first touch of each level, entries, lot exits, stops, stop moves, re-buys). Toggles in `janus_configs.py`, all OFF by default: `MACHINE_REBUY`, `MACHINE_COUNTER`, `MACHINE_TSL` (rules in `TSL_RULES`). `machine_run.py` runs one symbol (default CRUDEOILM, range-setter levels) and writes `outputs/machine/<run name>/` (gitignored, emptied at the start of each run): `trades.csv`, `legs.csv`, `events.csv`, `sessions.csv`, `minute/<trade_key>.csv`, `run_info.txt`. `machine_run.py --parity` checks the machine against `janus_engine.py` leg for leg. `--tsl --no-tsl-reentry` blocks a setup for the rest of the session after a trailed stop closed its trade (`TSL_REENTRY`); `--source previous_session` runs the control levels; `--tsl-rules entry,stay` sets where the stop goes after T1 and after T2 (places `entry`, `T1`, `T2`, `stay`; default `entry,T1`); the run folder name carries the level source, the toggles and any non-default trail rules (`tsl-on-noreentry_t1-entry_t2-stay`). Touch entries only for now.
- Tests: `tests/test_janus_machine.py`, `tests/test_janus_engine.py`, `tests/test_janus_setups.py`, `tests/test_janus_levels.py`, `tests/test_janus_events.py` (hand-computed levels, constructed price paths, mirror symmetry, benchmark arithmetic).

## How to read a first-passage result

After the first intraday touch of a level, the table gives the share of touches where the inward level came first, among touches where either was reached, next to the driftless benchmark (gambler's ruin from the level distances: for R3 with outward R4 and inward R2 the benchmark is 75% because R2 is three times closer). Excess over the benchmark is what matters, not the raw share. Negative excess means price continued outward more than a driftless walk would, positive means it reverted more.

## Phase 0 findings (2026-10-05, re-run 2026-10-07 with the filled 2026 Fyers void included: 884 to 1,383 sessions per instrument)

See `outputs/phase0_summary.txt` for every table. Headline: on all four instruments, after a touch of R3/S3 the inward level (R2/S2) is reached first less often than the driftless benchmark (excess -6 to -11 points), and after R4/S4 the continuation to R5/S5 beats the benchmark by a similar margin. The sign is the same in every instrument and almost every year, and it is largest in 2026. That leans toward continuation rather than reversion at these levels, but it is not yet an edge: it is not net of costs, and touching an extreme level selects trending days, so a plain "a day that reached an extreme tends to keep going" effect would show the same sign without any Camarilla-specific information. The shuffled-levels null in Phase 2 is what separates those.

R6/S6 (added 2026-10-05): touched intraday in only about 5% to 10% of sessions per side. After a touch of R5/S5 the move back to R4/S4 came before R6/S6 more often than not (42% to 45% of decided cases against a 54% driftless benchmark, so continuation again beats the benchmark by 9 to 12 points), but 26% to 41% of those touches end the session with neither level reached.

R7/S7 (added 2026-10-06, figures re-run 2026-10-07 with the void filled): touched intraday in about 1% to 5% of sessions per side (CRUDEOILM 2.5%/2.9%, SILVERMIC 4.4%/4.1%, GOLDPETAL 4.8%/4.1%, NATGASMINI 2.7%/1.0%). After a touch of R6/S6, R7/S7 was reached first in 28% to 44% of touches and R5/S5 first in 21% to 35%, and among decided cases the inward level came first 39% to 45% of the time against a 54% driftless benchmark, so continuation beats the benchmark by 9 to 15 points on every instrument. 23% to 51% of those touches end the session with neither level reached, and the samples are small (106 to 247 touches).
